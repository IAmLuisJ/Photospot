# Design context

Confirmed with Luis: the primary audience is photographers finding locations.
The golden path is browsing nearby spots, checking practical details, then
getting directions. The experience should be calm and photo-led.

Use a restrained field-guide aesthetic: warm paper, forest green, expressive
editorial headings, and generous space. Photos and place names lead. Advanced
filters, contribution tools, and community actions use progressive disclosure.
Browsing never requires an account. Mobile gets explicit photo/map controls,
without depending on hidden gestures. Preserve a visitor's place through sign-in.
